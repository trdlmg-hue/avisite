About Avisite

Avisite is a collection of free online tools and guides. We help people work with images, PDFs and study material without sign-ups, watermarks or hidden charges.

What we offer

  • Image and PDF tools that run in your browser.
  • Quiz maker that turns your study PDFs into a question bank, with practice and exam modes.
  • Guides with simple, step-by-step help.

Our promise

Most of our tools process your files on your own device. Files are not uploaded to our servers for those tools. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Install on your phone

You can add Avisite to your home screen and open it like an app. No app store is needed.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Avisite in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Install button at the bottom, or open the menu (⋮) and tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open Avisite in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
