Applications, college admissions, bank forms and office paperwork often ask for a single PDF file, not a pile of separate photos. Converting your images into one PDF keeps the pages in the right order, and it is much easier to email or upload. Here is how to do it in a few minutes.

## Step 1: Take clear photos

Place the document on a flat surface, in good light, and keep the phone straight above it. Avoid shadows and blur. Each page should fill most of the frame.

## Step 2: Put the images in order

Use [Image to PDF](/tools/image-to-pdf.html) to add all the photos at once. Use the Up and Down buttons to arrange them. Each image becomes one page, so the first photo is page one.

## Step 3: Choose the page settings

For most forms, A4 with a small margin works well. If you want each page to be exactly the size of its photo, choose **Fit to image**. For portrait documents, keep the orientation on automatic.

## Step 4: Keep the file small

Large phone photos can make a large PDF. If the file is too big for the portal, compress the photos with our [Image Compressor](/tools/image-compressor.html) first, using a width of about 1024 pixels. Then create the PDF again.

## Step 5: Check and download

Open the downloaded PDF and check every page. Make sure the text is readable and nothing is cut off. Then upload it to the form.

## Tips

- Name the output file clearly, such as *aadhaar-card.pdf*, so it is easy to find.
- If you need to join this PDF with other PDFs, use our [Merge PDF](/tools/merge-pdf.html) tool.
- Convert HEIC iPhone photos to JPG first, if your browser cannot open them.
