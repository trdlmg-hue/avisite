How to compress a PDF

  1. Choose the PDF from your phone or computer.
  2. Start with Keep text. If the file is still too big, try Smaller.
  3. For Smaller mode, pick a quality. Low gives the smallest file.
  4. Press Compress PDF, check the new size, and download it.

Which mode should I use?

  • Keep text: best when you need to search or copy text from the PDF, such as notes or reports. It removes extra data but keeps the pages as they are.
  • Smaller: best for scanned documents, forms and photos inside a PDF. Each page becomes a high-quality image, so the size drops a lot, but the text cannot be selected.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is compressed inside your browser, and it is not sent to Avisite's servers.

Will compressing a PDF reduce the text quality?

The Keep text mode keeps the text sharp and selectable. The Smaller mode turns each page into an image, which gives a much smaller file, but the text cannot be selected or searched.

How do I make a PDF under 100 KB or 200 KB?

Use Smaller mode with Low quality. If it is still big, reduce the number of pages, or compress the photos inside the PDF first with our Image Compressor.
