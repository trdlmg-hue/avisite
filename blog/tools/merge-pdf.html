    How to merge PDF files

    1. Choose two or more PDF files. You can select many at once.
    2. Arrange them with Up and Down. The first file comes first in the merged PDF.
    3. Enter a name, press Merge PDFs, and download the file.

    When is merging PDFs useful?

    Many applications and forms ask for a single PDF, but your documents are often split into several files, such as a scanned certificate, an ID proof and a mark sheet. Merging them into one file makes uploading and emailing much easier.

    If the merged file is too large, compress the images in it first, or split the PDF into smaller parts.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    You can merge up to 30 PDF files at once, with a total size of 150 MB.

    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

    No. The PDFs are merged inside your browser, and they are not sent to Avisite's servers.

    Why can't I merge a password-protected PDF?

    Password-protected PDFs cannot be read without the password. Remove the password in your PDF reader first, then merge the file here.
