How to compress an image

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your phone or computer.
  2. Pick the output format. JPG is usually the smallest for photos.
  3. Lower the quality slider a little, and set a maximum width if you need a smaller size.
  4. Press Compress image, check the new size, and download it.

Why compress images?

Many forms, job portals and websites ask for images below a certain size, such as 100 KB or 200 KB. Large photos from modern phones can be 3 to 8 MB, which is often too big for email, WhatsApp status or online applications. Compressing the image keeps the picture usable while making the file small enough to upload.

Reducing the width (for example to 800 px) also helps a lot, because a phone photo is often far bigger than a screen needs. For documents and scanned pages, you may also want to try the PDF tools on Avisite.

Which format should I choose?

  • JPG: best for photos, usually the smallest file.
  • WebP: smaller than JPG at similar quality. Works in all modern browsers and most apps.
  • PNG: best for screenshots, logos and images with transparent backgrounds. The file stays larger because PNG does not lose quality.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The image is compressed inside your browser, and it is not sent to Avisite's servers.

Which image formats can I compress?

You can upload JPG, PNG and WebP images. You can save the result as JPG, WebP or PNG.

Why is my compressed image not smaller?

Images that are already compressed may not get smaller. Try a lower quality, a smaller width, or the JPG or WebP format.

Why does my iPhone HEIC photo not open?

Some browsers do not read HEIC files. Change the photo to JPG in your phone's settings (Camera → Formats → Most Compatible), or convert it first, then upload the JPG here.
