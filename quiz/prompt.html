Create a quiz from your PDF

Use an AI tool like ChatGPT or Claude to turn your study material into a quiz file. Then upload it to Avisite and start practicing or take a timed exam.

Step 1: Enter pages and copy the prompt

Step 2: Upload your PDF to the AI

Open ChatGPT or Claude, attach your PDF, paste the copied prompt, and send. The AI will reply with the JSON.

Large PDF? Change the page numbers, for example pages 16 to 30, and repeat in the same chat. Save each reply as a separate file, and upload them all together on the start page.

Step 3: Save the JSON as a file

Copy only the JSON (starting with { and ending with }). Paste it into a plain text editor like Notepad, and save it as quiz.json. Do not add any extra text above or below it.

Step 4: Start your quiz

Go to quiz upload

Tips for a good quiz

  • Use clear, text-based PDFs. Scanned images may not be read properly.
  • Ask for fewer questions (like 20) if the PDF is short, or split a long PDF into parts.
  • Always check the answers once before taking an exam.
