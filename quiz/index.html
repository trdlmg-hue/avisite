Features

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to Avisite?

No. You upload the PDF to ChatGPT or Claude yourself. Avisite only reads the JSON file you upload here, and it stays in your browser.

Will my progress be saved?

Yes, in this browser. Clearing your browser data will remove saved progress and history.

Can I combine questions from several PDFs?

Yes. Upload several quiz files and they go into one question bank. Duplicate questions are skipped automatically.
