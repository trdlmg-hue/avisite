How to rotate, extract or delete PDF pages

  1. Choose a PDF file. The tool shows how many pages it has.
  2. Pick an action: rotate, extract or delete.
  3. Enter page numbers, such as 1-3, 5. For rotate, leave the box empty to turn every page.
  4. Press Make PDF and download the new file.

When is this useful?

A scanned page that was saved sideways, a form with one page you need alone, or a document with a few pages you do not want to share are common cases. This tool handles them without any software to install.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate only some pages of a PDF?

Yes. Enter page numbers such as 1-3, 5 to rotate only those pages. Leave the box empty to rotate every page.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The pages are changed inside your browser, and your file is not sent to Avisite's servers.

Can I delete all pages?

No. A PDF must keep at least one page, so the tool stops you if you try to delete every page.
