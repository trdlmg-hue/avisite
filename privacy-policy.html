Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Your files

Most of our tools, including image compression, PDF tools and the quiz maker, process your files inside your browser. Those files are not uploaded to our servers.

Data stored in your browser

The quiz feature saves your question bank, unfinished tests, test results and history in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing your browser data, or by using the clear options in the quiz pages.

Cookies and analytics

If we add analytics or advertising in future, this policy will be updated to say so before it goes live.

Third-party services

We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may collect basic request data when fonts are loaded.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit our contact page.
